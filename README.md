# photo_album_slider

A Flutter project to make use of older devices by making them into digital photo albums.

## Inspired by my children

I have deployed this on devices with a SD card full of photos as presents for loved ones
by simply sideloading the app.

It allows you to select as many photos from local storage as you like, and display them in a slide show.
Select from 4 different speeds or stop at anytime from the menu.  Remove an image from the slideshow
without ever leaving the show with use of the delete icon.
